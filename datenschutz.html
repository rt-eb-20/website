2. Hosting

This website is hosted via GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA (a company of Microsoft Corporation). Delivery is handled through a content delivery network (including Fastly). When the website is accessed, the hosting provider automatically collects information in so-called server log files that your browser transmits. These are, in particular:

  • IP address of the requesting device,
  • date and time of access,
  • name and URL of the file accessed,
  • amount of data transferred,
  • browser and operating system used,
  • referrer URL (the previously visited page).

This processing serves to ensure trouble-free operation and the security of the website. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (legitimate interest in the technically error-free presentation and optimisation of the website). In the course of this, data is also transferred to servers in the USA. GitHub, Inc. / Microsoft is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework; in addition, the EU Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses are used as appropriate safeguards within the meaning of Art. 44 et seq. GDPR.

4. Contact by email

If you contact me by email, the information you provide (email address, name, and the content of your message) is stored and processed for the purpose of handling your enquiry. 6. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the following rights:

  • access to the data processed (Art. 15 GDPR),
  • rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16 GDPR),
  • erasure (Art. 17 GDPR),
  • restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR),
  • data portability (Art. 20 GDPR),
  • objection to processing (Art. 21 GDPR).

An informal notice to the email address above is sufficient to exercise these rights. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR); the competent authority includes the State Commissioner for Data Protection of Saxony-Anhalt (Landesbeauftragter für den Datenschutz Sachsen-Anhalt).
